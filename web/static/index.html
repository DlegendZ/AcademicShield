About

🧠 XGBoost ⚡ <1 ms inference 🔒 100% browser 📊 0-4 GPA scale

Academic Shield predicts your burnout risk and estimated GPA from your daily habits and mental-health indicators.

Two XGBoost models power the predictions - one classifies burnout (Healthy/Mildly Burnout/Burnout), the other regresses your GPA on a 0-4 scale.

Everything runs 100% in your browser. No personal data is sent to any server during prediction.
